About

A short history

I’m interested in closing the loop between how models are trained and how ML research itself gets done.

At Thinking Machines Lab, I was the model captain for Inkling and started the reasoning team. I now lead Auto Research, working to automate ML research.

Previously, I worked on the Grok 4 family at xAI. Before that, I studied EECS at UC Berkeley, where I was advised by Ion Stoica and helped build LMArena.
